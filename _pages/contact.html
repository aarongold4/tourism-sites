EDITORIAL

Contact us

The right channels for reaching the team behind PetraTourism.com — editorial, corrections, partnerships, and accessibility questions.

Last reviewed June 5, 2026 · See our editorial standards

We read everything that comes in, even when we can't reply immediately. Use the appropriate address below to help your message reach the right person and get a useful response.

General inquiries

For most questions about the Site, our coverage, or general travel advice that isn't covered in our published pages: hello@petratourism.com.

Please be aware we cannot provide personalized trip planning, custom itinerary creation, or one-on-one travel consulting via email. The Site itself is designed to answer the questions most travelers have; if your specific question isn't covered, we may add it to our editorial backlog for future content but can't promise individual replies for general planning questions.

Editorial corrections

If you find a factual error, outdated information, or broken link on the Site, we genuinely want to hear about it. Send corrections to hello@petratourism.com with:

  • The page URL where you found the issue
  • A description of what's wrong
  • The correct information, if you have it, along with a source if possible

We review correction reports weekly and update affected pages promptly. Pages that we update display a revised "Last reviewed" date reflecting the correction.

Story tips and recommendations

If you've discovered something we've missed — a new hotel worth covering, a tour operator doing exceptional work, a hike or viewpoint not on our map, a practical travel tip we don't address — let us know at hello@petratourism.com.

We don't pay for story tips and we don't accept content submissions for publication (this is an editorial site, not a contributor network). But thoughtful suggestions inform our editorial calendar and we appreciate them.

Hotels, tour operators, and travel businesses

If you operate a tour, hotel, or travel-related business in the Petra/Jordan region and would like to be considered for editorial coverage, contact hello@petratourism.com with:

  • Your business name and website
  • A brief description of what you offer and what makes it distinctive
  • Pricing and capacity information
  • Anonymous booking access (we don't accept comped stays or tours for review purposes; we book and pay as regular customers)

Please note: editorial coverage is not for sale. We do not accept payment for inclusion in our coverage, and we do not allow pre-publication review of content by operators we cover. Operators are welcome to suggest themselves for consideration but we make independent editorial decisions about what to cover and how.

Press and media inquiries

For press inquiries, interview requests, or media partnership questions: hello@petratourism.com. Please include "PRESS" in the subject line.

We're happy to provide commentary on Petra and Jordan travel topics where our expertise is relevant. For purely promotional press releases unrelated to our editorial coverage, please direct them elsewhere.

Partnership and advertising inquiries

For advertising and partnership opportunities: hello@petratourism.com. Please include "PARTNERSHIP" in the subject line.

We accept display advertising through Google AdSense and participate in established affiliate programs (Booking.com, Viator, GetYourGuide, and similar). For direct advertising or sponsored content arrangements, please outline your proposal — we evaluate opportunities case by case and have specific standards for what we'll accept.

We do not accept sponsored content disguised as editorial, paid backlink schemes, or promotional content for businesses we haven't independently evaluated. We do not sell guest post placements.

Accessibility feedback

We aim to make the Site accessible to all users regardless of ability. If you encounter accessibility issues — screen reader incompatibilities, contrast or readability problems, navigation difficulties, or any other barriers to using the Site — please let us know at hello@petratourism.com.

Include "ACCESSIBILITY" in the subject line so the message reaches the right person quickly. We treat accessibility issues as priority items and respond within a few days.

Privacy and data requests

For privacy-related inquiries — questions about how we handle your data, requests to access or delete your personal information, or other rights under applicable data protection laws (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, etc.) — contact hello@petratourism.com with "PRIVACY" in the subject line.

See our privacy policy for full details on what data we collect and your rights regarding it.

Response times

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 5 business days, often faster for time-sensitive matters (corrections, accessibility issues). High-volume periods or particularly complex requests may take longer.

If you haven't heard back within two weeks and your inquiry is genuinely important, feel free to follow up. Email occasionally goes astray, and we'd rather hear from you twice than not at all.

What we can't help with

To set realistic expectations, we are not equipped to provide:

  • Personalized trip planning. The Site itself is our trip-planning resource; we can't build custom itineraries via email.
  • Booking services. We're an editorial publication, not a travel agency. We link to booking platforms but don't process bookings directly.
  • Real-time travel updates. For current border conditions, security situations, or weather, contact your government's travel advisory service.
  • Visa or document advice. For specific visa, passport, or entry-document questions, contact the relevant embassies or official immigration services.
  • Translation or interpretation services. We can't help with Arabic or other language assistance beyond what's published on our language page.
  • Refunds or disputes with travel operators. We can't intervene in disputes between you and hotels, tour operators, or other businesses. Contact those operators directly or use the consumer protection mechanisms in your jurisdiction.

Mailing address

Most inquiries are handled by email. For situations requiring a physical mailing address (legal correspondence, etc.), please email hello@petratourism.com to request the appropriate address; we'll provide it based on the nature of the inquiry.

Other ways to engage

We don't currently operate on social media platforms in any official capacity. References to "Petra Tourism" accounts on social media that aren't linked from this Site should be treated as unaffiliated — we're not behind them, and we can't vouch for their content.

If we add official social presence in the future, links will appear in the Site footer.
