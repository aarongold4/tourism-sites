Paragliding in Kruševo

World-championship thermals over the highest town in the Balkans — tandem flights from €60.

Ask European paraglider pilots about North Macedonia and watch the expression change: Kruševo is one of the continent's worshipped sites, a place where the geometry of ridge and plain manufactures thermals so reliable the world championships have come here to use them. For pilots that means cross-country distance days; for you it means the cheapest world-class tandem flight in Europe, over a town that already looks like a postcard from the ground.

What the flight is like

You meet the pilot, clip into a tandem harness on the launch slope above town, take a few accelerating steps as the wing inflates — and the ground simply resigns. The first minutes glide over Kruševo's stacked roofs and the Makedonium's white dome; with working thermals the pilot banks into the lift and the altimeter climbs, the Pelagonia plain unrolling toward Pelister's wall. Standard tandems run 15–30 minutes airborne; thermal days stretch longer. Landing is a walk-off in a field below, grin pre-installed.

Booking and prices

Local tandem operators fly spring through autumn, conditions permitting — and "conditions permitting" is the operative phrase: schedules flex around wind, so book a day or two ahead and keep flexibility in your own. Expect €60–80 for the standard flight, with GoPro footage packages and extended flights as add-ons; cash remains welcome. Morning and late-afternoon slots offer the smoothest air for nervous stomachs; midday offers the strongest lift for greedy ones.

Making the day

The flight pairs perfectly with Kruševo's ground game — Makedonium before, mountain-town lunch after — and slots into any central crossing (Prilep is 30 minutes; the Skopje–Ohrid drive bends past with minor guilt). If the wind cancels you, the town consoles well; if it doesn't, you'll spend the drive home explaining to companions why everyone doesn't do this.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a tandem flight in Kruševo?

Typically €60–80 for a standard tandem of roughly 15–30 airborne minutes depending on conditions, with longer thermal flights and photo/video packages above that — a fraction of Alpine tandem pricing for comparable (often better) air.

Why is Kruševo good for paragliding?

Geometry: the town's 1,350 m ridge drops to the sun-baked Pelagonia plain, generating reliable, well-organized thermals through the warm months — reliable enough that Kruševo has hosted world-championship competition. Pilots cross continents for air this consistent.

Is it safe for a first-timer?

Tandem flight with a certified local pilot is among the gentler ways to leave the ground — you run a few steps, sit into a harness, and the pilot does everything. Conditions rule the schedule: flights wait for the right wind, which is the safety system working.
