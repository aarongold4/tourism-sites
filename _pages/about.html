About

northmacedoniatourism.com is an independently published travel guide to North Macedonia. We are not affiliated with the government of North Macedonia, its national tourism agency or any tour operator.

Our goal is simple: the most accurate, useful and current English-language resource for planning a trip to the country — covering Lake Ohrid, Skopje and everywhere worth your time in between, with prices, timetables and opening hours verified before publication.

Every page notes when it was last updated. If you find something that has changed on the ground, we genuinely want to hear about it — see the contact page.
