Lake Ohrid Boat Tours

From €10 water-taxi hops to full-day cruises down the UNESCO shore.

Ohrid is a town that should be seen from the water at least once — the old town stacking up its hill, Kaneo's church on its cliff, the fortress crowning everything. The harbor runs a full menu of ways to do it, from ten-minute photo hops to all-day voyages down the UNESCO shore, and prices remain refreshingly pre-mass-tourism.

The short hops

Along the harbor front, boatmen offer 30–45 minute circuits of the town bay — out past the boardwalk, a slow drift beneath Kaneo, the old town panorama, home. Around €10 per person (negotiable off-peak, per-boat rates for groups), no booking needed, departures whenever a boat fills. As an orientation on arrival day it's hard to beat; as a sunset ride it's even better.

The St. Naum day

The flagship trip: down the full eastern shore to St. Naum Monastery, usually with a stop at the Bay of Bones stilt-village museum, three to four hours ashore for the monastery, springs and a swim, then the golden-hour cruise home — seven to eight hours all in. Walk-up shared boats charge about €15–25 return; packaged versions with guide, entrance fees and the springs rowboat bundled run €30–60 and remove all friction. Morning departures (around 9–10am) are the standard; take the earliest in summer.

Sunset cruises and charters

Evening sunset cruises — typically 1.5–2 hours with a drink included — have multiplied in recent seasons and earn their modest price on any clear evening; the light show over the Albanian mountains is reliable theatre. Private charters, from classic wooden boats to small speedboats, cost from roughly €50–100 for a private hour-plus depending on vessel and season, and unlock the quieter spots: Trpejca bay for a swim stop, the springs at golden hour, or simply drifting while the town lights come on.

Choosing well

Boats and operators vary more in vessel comfort than in route — they all pass the same magnificent shore. Check shade cover in high summer, confirm exactly what's included on St. Naum packages (the springs rowboat and church entry are the commonly omitted bits), and carry cash for walk-up deals. Mornings are calmer on the open water; afternoons breezier. And wherever you sail, be on deck for the Kaneo pass — the church from the water is the photograph you actually came for.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the boat to St. Naum?

Walk-up shared boats at Ohrid harbor run around €15–25 return at the going rate; organized tours with a guide, entries and the springs rowboat included typically cost €30–60 depending on group size and inclusions. Short Kaneo-and-bay hops cost about €10.

Do I need to book boat tours in advance?

In July–August, booking a day or two ahead is wise for the St. Naum day trips and sunset cruises; outside peak season you can walk the harbor and negotiate on the spot. Private charters always benefit from advance contact.

Is the lake rough?

Usually calm, especially mornings — afternoon breezes can put chop on the open crossing. The shared boats are slow, stable and sociable; anyone prone to seasickness will almost always be fine here.
