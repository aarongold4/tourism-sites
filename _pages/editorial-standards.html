EDITORIAL

Editorial standards

How we research, how we test, how we make decisions about what to recommend, and how we handle conflicts and corrections.

Last reviewed April 28, 2026 · See our editorial standards

Our purpose

PetraTourism.com is an independent travel publication covering Petra, Jordan, and the broader region around it. Our purpose is to be the most useful, accurate, and honest English-language resource for people planning a trip to Petra. We aren't a tour operator, a hotel chain, or an agent for any of the businesses we cover. We are editors and writers who pay our own way and report what we find.

Independence

The editorial team operates independently from any commercial relationship. Affiliate partners, advertisers, and the businesses we cover have no influence over what we publish, when we publish it, or how we recommend their products. We never accept payment in exchange for positive coverage, and businesses we review never see what we write before it is published.

When we link to a product or service that pays us a commission for bookings made through our links, we disclose this in our affiliate disclosure. The presence of an affiliate relationship does not change our editorial assessment. If a non-affiliated option is the better choice for our readers, that's what we recommend.

How we research

Every tour, hotel, and major attraction covered on PetraTourism.com has been visited firsthand by a member of our editorial team. Where we rely on outside expertise — local guides, archaeologists, hotel staff, government tourism offices — we name our sources or describe them clearly. We don't fabricate experiences and we don't pass off press releases as reporting.

Practical information (prices, opening hours, schedules, fees) is verified at the time of publication against the relevant primary source where one exists: the Petra Development and Tourism Region Authority for ticket and Jordan Pass information, JETT's official website for bus schedules, individual hotels and operators for their own pricing, the relevant border authorities for crossing logistics. Where multiple sources conflict, we say so.

How we test

For tours and hotels, we book anonymously through public-facing channels (Booking.com, Expedia, Viator, GetYourGuide, or direct websites), at full retail prices, the same way our readers would. We do not identify ourselves as journalists at the time of booking. On the rare occasion an operator hosts us — typically when we are testing a new product or experience for the first time — we disclose this clearly at the top of the relevant review.

We do not accept gifts, free upgrades, or extended hospitality in exchange for coverage. We do not accept paid placement, sponsored posts, or any other form of compensated editorial content. We do not allow operators to review, edit, or approve our coverage before publication.

Update cadence

Tourism prices, schedules, and policies change. Every page on PetraTourism.com displays a "Last reviewed" date that reflects the most recent time the page was checked against current source material. We do a full sweep of every monetized page (tours, hotels, transport guides) at least every six months. We update pages more frequently when we have direct ground-truth intelligence — a price change observed during a visit, a schedule change announced by a transport provider, a policy change announced by the tourism authority.

When a page has not been reviewed in over twelve months, we either update it or mark it with a notice indicating that information may be out of date.

Corrections

We aim to be accurate, but we will sometimes get things wrong. When we discover an error — through our own review, a reader's report, or a primary source's correction — we update the relevant page and add a correction notice at the bottom. For significant corrections (a factual claim that materially changes a recommendation), we date and describe the change. For minor corrections (a typo, a small numerical update), we simply update.

If you spot an error, please write to corrections@petratourism.com. We read every message even when we can't reply individually.

Use of AI tools

Our writers use AI tools (including large language models) in the same way we use search engines, calculators, and reference databases — as research aids, not as content generators. Every published page on PetraTourism.com is written, edited, and reviewed by a human member of our editorial team. We do not publish AI-generated text as final copy, and we do not represent AI-generated content as our own firsthand reporting. Where AI tools are used to draft, summarize, or organize material, the output is reviewed and rewritten by a human before publication.

Contact

For editorial concerns, source disputes, correction requests, or any question about how we work, write to editor@petratourism.com.
