GETTING THERE

Getting to Saqqara and Dahshur

The best archaeological day near Cairo has exactly one logistical catch: no practical public transport and no waiting taxis. The fix is simple — a vehicle that stays with you — and it comes in three honest formats.

Last reviewed June 6, 2026◆5-minute read

Photo: Radosław Botev · CC BY 3.0 pl · Wikimedia Commons

Giza forgives transport improvisation — ride-hail in, shuttle inside, ride-hail out. The southern sites don't: Saqqara, Memphis, and Dahshur sit along the desert edge 25-45 minutes south, spread across a triangle with no useful buses, no metro, and — the decisive fact — no idle taxis or app cars waiting at desert sites for your next leg. The day's infrastructure is a vehicle that stays with you, and the only real question is which of three formats provides it.

Format one: the hired car-and-driver

The classic solution and the self-guided traveler's pick: an air-conditioned car with a driver who knows the circuit, waits at each site, and handles the lunch stop — roughly $40-70 for the vehicle for the day at current rates, split among however many you are. Sources, in rough order of price: pre-booked online transfer services, hotel desks (convenient, marked up — agree the full itinerary and price in writing), and the informal market of drivers who do this run weekly. The terms to fix before rolling: the three sites by name (Dahshur gets quietly dropped from lazy quotes), waiting time included, the lunch venue's commission status (why you ask), and one total number. Tip at day's end — the southern circuit is honest work.

Format two: the private tour

The same vehicle with an Egyptologist aboard — the format reviewed in full at the three-site day and private tiers. At Saqqara specifically, the guide premium buys more than usual: the tombs reward narration, and operators know which are open this season. Roughly $60-100 per person all formats considered, fees separate.

Format three: the ride-hail day-rate play

The budget hack that actually works: take an Uber or Careem south, and before leaving the city — or at pickup — convert it into a day arrangement at a fixed waiting rate; many drivers welcome the guaranteed fare. Agree the same terms as a hired car (sites, waiting, one total, cash), and accept the trade-offs: no vetting beyond the app profile, variable English, and the app's protections only covering the metered legs. It reliably undercuts the hotel desk; whether it undercuts a pre-booked driver depends on your negotiating morning. What doesn't work: point-to-point app rides between the sites — the return legs strand you.

Route logistics, whichever format

The drives are short — Giza to Saqqara 25-30 minutes, Saqqara to Memphis 15-20, Memphis to Dahshur 15-20 — and the standard order (timed in full here) runs Saqqara at opening, Memphis at lunch, Dahshur early afternoon, back ahead of the Cairo peak (the time-of-day matrix). Each site tickets separately, card-first; carry water and small bills; and at Saqqara, use the vehicle between tomb clusters — the site is bigger than its map suggests, and the energy you save is Dahshur's Red Pyramid descent.

Frequently asked questions

Is there any public transport to Saqqara or Dahshur?

Nothing practical. Microbuses serve the villages near Saqqara with a long final walk and no onward link to Dahshur; nothing useful serves Dahshur at all. The southern day runs on a vehicle that waits — hired driver, private tour, or a day-rate ride-hail arrangement.

What does a driver for the day cost?

A car-and-driver covering Saqqara, Memphis, and Dahshur from Cairo or Giza runs roughly $40-70 for the vehicle at current rates — arranged through your hotel, pre-booked online, or negotiated with a ride-hail driver as a fixed day rate. Tickets, lunch, and the driver's tip ride on top.

Can I use Uber to get there?

Getting there, yes; getting around and back, no — desert-edge sites have no idle cars, and you'd be stranded between stops. The workable app version is converting the ride into a day arrangement: many drivers will quote a fair waiting rate for the circuit. Agree it as one total before leaving the city.

How far apart are the sites?

Conveniently close: Saqqara is 25-30 minutes south of Giza, Memphis 15-20 minutes from Saqqara, Dahshur another 15-20 south. The full circuit from central Cairo and back is about 90-110 minutes of total driving spread across the day.
