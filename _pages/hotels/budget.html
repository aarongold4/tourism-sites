Best budget hotels in Petra

Under $60 USD per night in Wadi Musa is genuinely possible — and the best budget options still deliver everything you need. Here's where to actually find value at the low end and where the savings stop being savings.

Last reviewed April 28, 2026 · 6-minute read · See our editorial standards

The budget tier in Wadi Musa runs from $12 USD dorm beds to $60 USD private rooms with ensuite bathrooms. For backpackers, solo travelers, and budget-conscious families, this tier delivers real value — the basics (clean rooms, hot water, included breakfast, walking distance to the gate at least in some cases) are reliably available below $50 USD per night. The trade-offs are predictable: older properties, more variable service, longer walks to the gate, fewer amenities than the mid-range options.

This page covers the most consistently recommended budget options, the realistic trade-offs at each price point, and where the actual best value sits in the Wadi Musa budget market. The framing throughout: budget travel in Wadi Musa works well, but you need to make informed choices about which compromises matter and which don't.

The four most-recommended budget options

Valentine Inn. About 800m from the Petra gate (the closest of the true budget options). Mix of dorms ($15-25 USD/night) and private rooms ($30-50 USD/night). Family-run, welcoming, includes a budget dinner option as well as breakfast. The strongest combination of location and price at the budget tier.

Cleopetra Hotel. About 1.5 km from the gate in Wadi Musa town. Private rooms only, $35-55 USD per night. Older property but consistently well-maintained. The breakfast is widely praised. Closer to local restaurants than to the Petra entrance.

Sharah Mountains Hotel. About 1 km from the gate, on a side road off Tourism Street. Private rooms $40-60 USD per night, simple but comfortable, mountain-facing rooms catch nice morning light. Good fallback option when Valentine Inn is full.

Rocky Mountain Hotel. About 1.5 km from the gate in upper Wadi Musa. Dorms and private rooms, $12-45 USD per night range. The cheapest reliable option in town. Trade-off is the longer walk and a more bare-bones experience than Valentine Inn delivers at slightly higher price.

The decision: location vs price

At the budget tier, the key trade-off is location vs absolute lowest price. The cheapest options (Rocky Mountain Hotel dorms at $12 USD) sit 1.5 km from the Petra gate, which means a 20-25 minute walk uphill at the end of a tired Petra day or a 3-5 JOD taxi. Over a 2-3 night stay, the daily taxi costs ($6-15 USD total) start to erode the price advantage over a $30-45 USD per night room at Valentine Inn that you can walk to.

Our practical recommendation for most budget travelers: Valentine Inn private room. The 800m walk to the gate is meaningful, the price ($30-50 USD) is genuinely budget, the family-run feel is warm, and you avoid daily taxi friction. The dorm option at Valentine Inn is even cheaper for solo travelers who don't mind shared sleeping.

What you give up at the budget tier

Worth understanding the realistic compromises:

  • Room quality variance. Budget Wadi Musa hotels are typically older properties with cosmetic wear (worn furniture, faded textiles, dated bathroom fixtures). The basics work; the aesthetics don't impress.
  • Service variance. Family-run service is warmer but inconsistent. One traveler's check-in might be excellent and another's average.
  • AC reliability. Air conditioning is present but sometimes underpowered for genuine July heat. Look for recent summer reviews specifically.
  • Breakfast variety. Some budget hotels serve genuinely good Jordanian breakfasts; others offer minimal continental spreads. Variable.
  • Limited common spaces. No pool, no lounge to speak of, no on-site bar. Common areas tend to be functional rather than welcoming.
  • Sometimes-spotty Wi-Fi. The Wadi Musa cellular network has gotten better, but in-room Wi-Fi at budget hotels can be slow or intermittent.

None of these are deal-breakers for budget-conscious travelers. The point is to know what to expect and not pay mid-range prices hoping for budget-tier results.

The hostel dorm experience in Wadi Musa

For solo backpackers, the hostel dorm option is genuinely good value. Valentine Inn and Rocky Mountain Hotel both offer mixed and female-only dorms in the $12-25 USD per night range. The dorm rooms typically hold 4-8 beds, with lockers, shared bathrooms, and common kitchens or lounges. Both properties attract a young international backpacker crowd, which means easy company for solo travelers but limited quiet hours.

What's good: the social atmosphere genuinely makes solo travel less lonely, the price is unbeatable, and the staff at both hostels are notably welcoming. What's not: privacy is minimal, noise levels in dorm rooms can be challenging for light sleepers, and the security situation depends on individual locker discipline.

When budget isn't the right choice

Three scenarios where the savings stop making sense:

1. Mobility constraints. Most budget Wadi Musa hotels have stairs, no elevators, and limited accessibility infrastructure. The walk to the gate matters even more for travelers with mobility issues. Better to spend the extra money at the Petra Moon Hotel or higher.

2. Tight schedule. If you're doing a one-day Petra visit and need predictable logistics, the variable budget-tier service can introduce friction at the wrong moments. Mid-range hotels are more predictable.

3. You want amenities between Petra days. A pool, a real restaurant, a proper hotel lounge — these are absent at the budget tier. If you want any of them, look at mid-range or higher.

Booking advice

Book direct or via Booking.com with free cancellation. Direct booking through hotel email is sometimes 10-15% cheaper than booking platforms, but loses the buyer protection. Booking.com's free-cancellation policy is worth the small markup for trips booked more than 14 days out.

Read recent reviews specifically. Budget Wadi Musa hotels evolve more than mid-range or luxury properties — a hotel that was excellent two years ago may have declined; one that was rough may have renovated. The last 30 days of reviews are the most reliable signal.

Check the specific room. Budget hotels often have a mix of renovated and older rooms. Booking platforms sometimes let you select specific room types — pay the small premium for a renovated room over a "standard" one when the option exists.

Frequently asked questions

How cheap can you stay in Wadi Musa?
The cheapest dorm beds run $12-18 USD per night in low season. Private budget rooms with shared bathrooms run $25-40 USD per night. Private rooms with ensuite bathrooms in budget hotels run $35-60 USD per night. For solo travelers or backpackers, the dorm-bed option is comparable to budget accommodations anywhere else in Jordan; for couples and families, the private-room budget hotels offer real value.
Which budget hotels are most consistently recommended?
Valentine Inn, Cleopetra Hotel, Sharah Mountains Hotel, and Rocky Mountain Hotel. All four sit in the $25-50 USD per night range, have 4+ star averages on Booking.com, and consistently appear in budget-traveler recommendations. The trade-offs are typical of the price tier — older properties, more variable service, longer walks to the gate — but the basics (clean rooms, hot water, breakfast included on most rates) are reliably delivered.
Are budget Wadi Musa hotels too far from the Petra gate?
Yes for most of them — the cheapest options sit 1-2 km uphill from the Visitor Centre, which means a 15-25 minute walk or a 3-5 JOD taxi to the gate. For the 06:00 Treasury entry strategy, taxis are the practical answer. Two budget hotels are exceptions: Valentine Inn (about 800m from the gate, walkable) and Sharah Mountains Hotel (similar). For travelers prioritizing walking distance to the gate over absolute lowest price, these are the right targets.
Is breakfast included at budget Wadi Musa hotels?
Usually yes, but the quality varies more than at mid-range hotels. Some budget hotels serve genuinely good Jordanian breakfasts; others offer continental-style spreads (bread, cheese, instant coffee) that are functional but uninspiring. Reviews specifically mentioning the breakfast quality are the most reliable signal. When in doubt, look for "great breakfast" appearing multiple times in recent reviews.
Should I book a hostel dorm or a budget private room?
Dorms work well for solo travelers comfortable with shared sleeping spaces — Valentine Inn and Rocky Mountain Hotel both have well-reviewed dorms in the $12-25 USD per night range. For couples, the per-person math usually flips toward private rooms — a $40 USD private double sleeps two for $20 each, similar to dorm rates with more privacy. For families with kids, private rooms are essentially required; family rooms (4 beds) are typically $50-70 USD per night.
