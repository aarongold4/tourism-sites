Cookie policy

How we use cookies and similar tracking technologies on PetraTourism.com — what they do, why we use them, and how to control them.

Last reviewed June 5, 2026 · See our editorial standards

Effective date: June 5, 2026. Last updated: June 5, 2026.

This Cookie Policy explains how PetraTourism.com uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit the Site. It complements our Privacy Policy by providing specific detail about cookie usage.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device and remember information about your visit — your preferences, your activity on the site, and similar data. Cookies are widely used to make websites function efficiently and to provide information to site owners.

The cookies on PetraTourism.com fall into several categories based on their purpose, duration, and provider.

Categories of cookies we use

1. Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for the Site to function and cannot be disabled without breaking core functionality. They include:

  • Session cookies that maintain your current session state as you navigate between pages
  • Security cookies that help prevent malicious activity and protect site integrity
  • Cookie consent cookies that remember your privacy preferences across visits

Strictly necessary cookies are exempt from consent requirements under EU and UK law because they are required for site operation.

2. Analytics cookies

We use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the Site, which helps us improve the content and user experience. These cookies collect aggregate, anonymized information about visitor behavior — pages visited, time on site, navigation paths, referring sources, and similar metrics.

The primary analytics service we use is Google Analytics 4 (GA4). Google Analytics cookies include:

  • _ga: Distinguishes individual users for aggregate analysis. Expires after 2 years.
  • _ga_[CONTAINER-ID]: Persists session state for Google Analytics 4. Expires after 2 years.
  • _gid: Distinguishes users for aggregate analysis. Expires after 24 hours.
  • _gat: Throttles request rate to limit data collection. Expires after 1 minute.

Google's own privacy policy governs Google's use of analytics data: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

3. Advertising cookies

We display advertising on certain pages of the Site through Google AdSense and may use additional advertising partners in the future. Advertising cookies serve several purposes:

  • Showing relevant advertisements based on your interests
  • Limiting the number of times you see the same advertisement
  • Measuring the effectiveness of advertising campaigns
  • Detecting and preventing fraudulent click activity

Common Google AdSense cookies include:

  • __gads: Used for ad targeting and measurement. Persists for 13 months.
  • IDE: Used to register user actions for ad effectiveness measurement. Persists for 13 months.
  • NID: Stores user preferences for ad customization. Persists for 6 months.

You can manage your Google ad personalization preferences at: https://adssettings.google.com. You can opt out of personalized advertising from many other companies through the Network Advertising Initiative: https://optout.networkadvertising.org.

4. Affiliate tracking cookies

When you click affiliate links to partner sites (Booking.com, Viator, GetYourGuide, and similar services), those partners may set cookies on your device to track referrals and attribute commissions appropriately. These cookies are set by the partner sites, not by PetraTourism.com directly.

Affiliate cookies typically include:

  • Referral identifier cookies: Track that you came to the partner site from our affiliate link.
  • Attribution window cookies: Maintain referral attribution for a defined period (typically 30-90 days) so that we receive credit if you complete a purchase after a delay.

Each affiliate partner operates under its own privacy policy. We do not receive personal information about your activity on partner sites through these cookies — only aggregate referral and commission data.

5. Functionality cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your language preference or recent searches. We use minimal functionality cookies; most Site features work without them.

Cookie consent

For visitors from the European Union (EU), the European Economic Area (EEA), and the United Kingdom (UK), we display a cookie consent banner on your first visit to the Site. The banner allows you to:

  • Accept all cookies: Enable all cookie categories above.
  • Reject non-essential cookies: Allow only strictly necessary cookies; block analytics, advertising, and functionality cookies.
  • Manage preferences: Selectively enable or disable specific cookie categories.

Your consent preferences are stored in a cookie and respected across the Site. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie preferences" link in the Site footer.

For visitors from other jurisdictions (including the United States), we operate on an "opt-out" model where cookies are enabled by default and you can disable them through your browser settings or through the opt-out mechanisms described above.

Managing cookies through your browser

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • View cookies stored on your device
  • Block all cookies, or block specific cookies from specific sites
  • Delete cookies (existing cookies, or all cookies when you close the browser)
  • Set notifications for when sites attempt to set cookies

The exact procedure depends on your browser. Documentation for major browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Chrome cookie settings
  • Mozilla Firefox: Firefox cookie settings
  • Apple Safari: Safari cookie settings
  • Microsoft Edge: Edge cookie settings

Note that disabling cookies may affect Site functionality. Some features may not work properly without cookies enabled, and you may need to re-enter information each visit.

Do Not Track signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting that allows users to indicate they don't wish to be tracked across websites. The web industry has not adopted a consistent standard for honoring DNT signals, and we currently do not respond to them. We provide other means for managing your tracking preferences as described above.

Updates to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page
  • Display a notification on the Site for material changes
  • Request renewed consent where required by applicable law
