GETTING THERE

The JETT bus to Petra

The cheapest, most reliable scheduled transport between Amman and Petra. One bus a day in each direction, 3.5 hours, 10-11 JOD. Here's everything you need to know to book and ride.

Last reviewed April 28, 2026 · 6-minute read · See our editorial standards

The JETT bus (Jordan Express Tourist Transport) operates the only scheduled, reliable coach service between Amman and Petra. For budget travelers and anyone without a rental car, it's the default transport choice — the cheapest option ($14-17 USD one-way), the most predictable (modern coaches, fixed schedule), and the simplest (direct drop-off at the Petra Visitor Centre, no onward connections needed). The single significant limitation is the once-daily schedule: one bus from Amman in the morning, one bus back from Petra in the late afternoon. Miss either, and you're paying for taxis or rental cars instead.

This page covers the 2026 schedule, current pricing, the booking process, and what to expect at both ends of the journey. We also cover the realistic alternatives if the JETT schedule doesn't fit your trip.

The 2026 schedule

The current schedule, as of April 2026:

  • Amman → Petra: 06:30 departure from Abdali Station, arriving Wadi Musa Visitor Centre approximately 10:00. Total journey time: 3.5 hours.
  • Petra → Amman: 17:00 departure from Wadi Musa Visitor Centre, arriving Amman Abdali approximately 20:30. Total journey time: 3.5 hours.

One bus daily in each direction. Both legs run year-round, including holidays. There is no second daily service — if you miss the bus, the alternatives are taxi or rental car. The schedule is fixed; we have not seen it change in the last 18 months of monitoring.

For Ramadan periods specifically, JETT sometimes adjusts the schedule slightly (later morning departures, earlier evening returns). Check jett.com.jo within a week of travel for Ramadan-period trips.

The route

The Amman-Petra route runs south along the Desert Highway (Route 15) for approximately 230 km. The journey is straightforward — long stretches of open desert with occasional small towns, one rest stop at approximately the halfway point (typically a fuel station with a snack shop and toilets), and the final approach into Wadi Musa via the King's Highway connector. The buses don't deviate from this route or make hotel pickup stops; you board at Abdali and you disembark at Wadi Musa Visitor Centre.

The Desert Highway is well-maintained and the route is geographically uneventful. Most travelers sleep, read, or look at the changing landscape. There's no major scenic stop or photographic interest along the way that justifies anything other than the direct route the JETT takes.

Booking

Three booking paths:

1. Online at jett.com.jo. The official JETT website. English-language interface, accepts international credit cards, sends an email confirmation that serves as your ticket. The site is functional but somewhat dated — sometimes timeout issues during peak booking periods. Most travelers should start here.

2. In person at Abdali Station. The JETT office is open during normal business hours (08:00-20:00 typically). Cash or card payment, immediate ticket issuance. Useful for travelers who haven't booked ahead or whose online booking failed.

3. Through your Amman hotel. Most hotels can arrange the booking for a small markup (2-3 JOD). Useful for travelers who'd rather not deal with the JETT website. The hotel handles the booking and provides the confirmation.

Our practical recommendation: Book online 1-3 days ahead. Same-day bookings are sometimes possible but the bus does sell out in peak season, particularly the morning Amman-Petra direction. Booking 2-3 days ahead is the safest play; booking more than a week ahead is unnecessary for the JETT specifically (rates don't drop further with advance purchase).

Boarding at Abdali Station

The Abdali JETT terminal is in the Abdali development of central Amman, on Khalil Salem Street. Most central Amman hotels (downtown, Jabal Amman, Sweifieh) are 5-15 minutes by taxi from Abdali, costing 1-3 JOD. Allow 30 minutes for traffic to make the 06:00 check-in for the 06:30 departure — Amman morning traffic can be heavier than expected, particularly during work-week rush.

The terminal itself is modest — a small indoor waiting area with seating, a ticket counter, and a snack kiosk. Restrooms are available. You'll need your printed ticket (or email confirmation on your phone) and your passport for the boarding process. Luggage goes in the coach's underneath storage compartment — there's no separate baggage fee.

Arrival at Petra

The bus drops at the Petra Visitor Centre parking area, approximately 100 meters from the main ticket booth. From there:

  • To the Mövenpick Resort or Petra Guest House: 2-5 minute walk.
  • To Petra Moon Hotel: 5-7 minute walk uphill.
  • To Wadi Musa town hotels (Petra Canyon, Cleopetra, etc.): 3-5 JOD taxi from the Visitor Centre, or a 15-20 minute uphill walk.

Taxis are typically waiting at the drop-off point. Standard local rates apply (negotiate or insist on the meter for taxi rides within Wadi Musa — most Wadi Musa taxis don't use meters in practice but the rate norms are well-established).

Other JETT routes

Beyond Amman-Petra, JETT also runs:

  • Petra ↔ Wadi Rum: Daily service, approximately 22 JOD one-way, 1.5-2 hours.
  • Petra ↔ Aqaba: Daily service, approximately 22 JOD one-way, 2 hours.
  • Amman ↔ Aqaba: Direct service, 4-5 hours.
  • Amman ↔ Dead Sea (Sweimeh): Daily service.

The Petra-Wadi Rum-Aqaba routes are particularly useful for travelers doing the classic Jordan loop without a rental car. Check jett.com.jo for current schedules on these routes — they update somewhat more frequently than the headline Amman-Petra service.

Alternatives if the JETT schedule doesn't work

The once-daily schedule is the JETT's biggest limitation. If you need to leave Amman after 06:30 or leave Petra before 17:00, the alternatives are:

Shared taxi from Abdali area. 25-35 JOD per person, fills slowly (sometimes 30+ minutes wait), runs throughout the morning. Cheaper than private but slower and less predictable than JETT.

Private taxi. 70-100 JOD for the whole car, runs whenever you want it. Best per-person economics for groups of 3-4 (16-25 JOD per person for the car). Arrange through your hotel.

Rental car. $30-45 USD per day plus fuel. Gives you full schedule flexibility and access to side detours (Karak Castle, Madaba on the King's Highway alternative route). Best for travelers doing more than just Petra in Jordan.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the JETT bus from Amman to Petra cost?
10-11 JOD ($14-17 USD) one-way; round-trip approximately 20 JOD ($28 USD). The bus stops at the Petra Visitor Centre directly, no onward connection needed. Children under 5 are free; ages 5-12 are typically half-price.
What's the JETT bus schedule for 2026?
Amman to Petra: 06:30 departure from Abdali Station, arriving Wadi Musa around 10:00. Petra to Amman: 17:00 departure from Petra Visitor Centre, arriving Amman around 20:30. The schedule is once-daily in each direction — there is no second bus. This makes booking 1-2 days ahead essential during peak season (March-May and September-November), and somewhat advisable year-round.
How do I book a JETT bus ticket?
Three options. (1) Online at jett.com.jo — the official site, takes English-language credit card payment, sends an email confirmation that serves as your ticket. (2) In person at the Abdali Station JETT office in Amman — cash or card, immediate ticket issuance. (3) Through your Amman hotel — most hotels can arrange the booking for a small markup (typically 2-3 JOD). For most travelers, online booking is the simplest path.
Where does the JETT bus depart from in Amman?
The Abdali Station in central Amman — specifically the JETT terminal at the Abdali development. Address is on Khalil Salem Street. Most central Amman hotels are 5-15 minutes by taxi (1-3 JOD) from Abdali. Allow 30 minutes for traffic to make the 06:00 check-in for the 06:30 departure. Coming from north of central Amman, factor in additional time.
Where does the JETT bus arrive at Petra?
Directly at the Petra Visitor Centre area. The drop-off point is at the front of the main parking area, about 100 meters from the ticket booth. From there, you can walk to most Visitor Centre area hotels (Mövenpick Resort, Petra Guest House) in under 5 minutes. For hotels further into Wadi Musa town (Petra Moon, Petra Canyon, Sella), a 3-5 JOD taxi from the drop-off point is needed.
Is the JETT bus comfortable?
Yes, by international coach standards. Air-conditioned, assigned seating with seatbelts, decent legroom, a single restroom stop on the 3.5-hour journey. The vehicles are modern Mercedes or Volvo touring coaches. WiFi is sometimes available, often spotty — don't plan to work productively for the duration. The ride itself is uneventful on the Desert Highway.
What if I miss the morning bus or want to leave Petra earlier?
No backup. The 06:30 Amman-Petra and 17:00 Petra-Amman are the only direct JETT services. If you miss the morning bus from Amman, alternatives are: shared taxi from the Abdali Station area (~25-35 JOD per person, fills slowly), private taxi (~70-100 JOD for the whole car), or rental car. If you want to leave Petra before 17:00, the options are the same — private taxi to Amman or south to Aqaba. There is no JETT-bus flexibility within a given day.
