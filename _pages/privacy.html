Privacy policy

How NorthMacedoniaTourism.com uses cookies, Google Analytics, Google AdSense and affiliate links, and how to opt out.

Last updated November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information NorthMacedoniaTourism.com (“we”, “the site”) collects when you visit, how it is used, and the choices you have. The site is an independent travel guide to Ohrid, Skopje and the rest of North Macedonia. We do not sell personal information.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider logs standard technical information for each visit: IP address, browser type, device type, the page requested, the referring page and the time of the request. These logs are used for security and to keep the site running.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are read and how visitors find the site. Google Analytics sets cookies and reports information such as pages viewed, approximate location (country/city), device type and time on page to us in aggregate. We do not use it to identify individual visitors.

Affiliate links

Some buttons and links on the site go to booking partners such as GetYourGuide, Viator and Booking.com. If you click one, the partner may set a cookie so that a booking you make can be credited to this site. Those partners' own privacy policies govern what they collect on their sites.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. We don't add you to any list and don't share your email with advertisers or affiliate partners.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to its use. To make a request, email hello@northmacedoniatourism.com with “Privacy” in the subject line. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as pages visited and time on site; this data is reported to us in aggregate. You can prevent Google Analytics from using your data with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
