Multi-day Petra tours

The way most international visitors actually experience Jordan — a tour package combining Petra with Wadi Rum, the Dead Sea, Jerash, and other major sites across 3-10 days. Here's the practical breakdown of formats, pricing, and how to choose.

Most international visitors to Petra don't visit Petra alone — they experience Petra as part of a broader Jordan trip combining the major regional destinations. Multi-day tour packages are the most common way to organize this kind of trip, providing the logistics (driver, vehicle, hotels, entrance fees, guide) as a coordinated package rather than requiring travelers to handle each element independently. For first-time Jordan visitors, multi-day tours are typically the path of least resistance to a full Jordan experience.

This page covers what multi-day tours actually include, the major format options (length, inclusions, accommodation tier), the cost ranges, and the practical decisions around booking. The framing throughout: the standard pattern for non-budget international travelers visiting Jordan, with both shared-group and private versions at different price points.

The standard formats

3-day Petra and Wadi Rum

The minimum-viable multi-day Jordan tour. Typically structured as:

  • Day 1: Pickup from Amman airport or hotel, drive to Petra (3-4 hours), afternoon visit to Petra, overnight in Wadi Musa.
  • Day 2: Full second day at Petra (Monastery climb, Royal Tombs, lower city), late-afternoon drive to Wadi Rum, overnight Bedouin camp.
  • Day 3: Sunrise tour and breakfast at Wadi Rum, drive back to Amman or onward to Aqaba/Eilat border.

Pricing: $400-700 USD per person, double occupancy, mid-range hotels. Premium versions with luxury Wadi Rum camps run higher.

5-day Petra, Wadi Rum, and Dead Sea

The most popular multi-day format. Adds the Dead Sea to the basic Petra+Wadi Rum trip, plus extra time at each destination:

  • Days 1-2: Amman base, including airport pickup, brief Amman tour, and Jerash day trip.
  • Days 2-3: Transfer to Petra (via Madaba and the King's Highway), 2 days at Petra.
  • Day 4: Drive to Wadi Rum, jeep tour and overnight camp.
  • Day 5: Wadi Rum sunrise, drive to Dead Sea (~4 hours), Dead Sea afternoon and overnight, return to Amman next day.

Pricing: $750-1,400 USD per person, double occupancy. The Dead Sea hotels add significant cost on the upper end if premium properties are included.

7-day full Jordan loop

The comprehensive option for travelers wanting to see most of the country:

  • Days 1-2: Amman, Jerash, Ajloun Castle (the northern destinations).
  • Day 3: Madaba (with the Byzantine mosaic map), Mount Nebo, the King's Highway scenic route to Petra.
  • Days 4-5: Two days at Petra.
  • Day 6: Wadi Rum jeep tour and overnight camp.
  • Day 7: Dead Sea morning, return to Amman.

Pricing: $1,100-2,200 USD per person, double occupancy. The most balanced format for first-time visitors wanting to see Jordan thoroughly.

10-day comprehensive

Adds additional destinations and slower pacing to the 7-day format — typically including Aqaba, additional time at Wadi Rum and Petra, optional side trips (Wadi Mujib, Shobak Castle, additional Dead Sea time, or specialized destinations).

Pricing: $1,500-3,500 USD per person, double occupancy. The right format for travelers with significant time budget and interest in deeper exploration.

What's included

Standard mid-range package inclusions

  • Round-trip airport transfers (Queen Alia Amman or Aqaba)
  • Private vehicle and English-speaking driver throughout the trip
  • Licensed guide for in-site interpretation at major archaeological sites
  • Accommodation at 4-star hotels with breakfast included
  • Bedouin camp overnight in Wadi Rum (basic or comfort tier)
  • All entrance fees to major sites (Petra, Jerash, Wadi Rum, etc.) or Jordan Pass
  • Some lunches at scheduled restaurants (often 50-70% of meals)
  • Bottled water throughout

Standard exclusions

  • International flights to/from Jordan
  • Travel insurance
  • Visa fees (typically $60 single entry, or covered by Jordan Pass with 2+ night stay)
  • Drinks at meals (water/coffee/tea may be included, alcohol almost never)
  • Lunches and dinners not specifically included
  • Optional activities (helicopter tours, hot springs, additional excursions)
  • Gratuities for drivers and guides (typically 30-50 USD per traveler for a full multi-day trip)
  • Personal expenses (laundry, room service, etc.)

Luxury tier additions

Premium packages typically add:

  • 5-star hotels throughout (Four Seasons Amman, Mövenpick Petra Resort, premium Wadi Rum camps, Kempinski Ishtar Dead Sea)
  • All meals included at top-tier restaurants
  • Senior-level guide
  • Premium vehicle (Mercedes V-Class, Range Rover, etc.)
  • Additional inclusions (helicopter tours, spa treatments, special-access cultural experiences)

Private vs shared group tours

Shared group tours

8-15 people in a coach or large van, fixed itinerary, scheduled departure dates. Pricing 30-40% cheaper than equivalent private tours. Best for:

  • Solo travelers and couples on tighter budgets
  • Travelers who enjoy the social aspect of traveling with others
  • Travelers comfortable with a fixed pace
  • First-time Jordan visitors who don't have specific customization needs

Private tours

Just your party (family, couple, group of friends) with dedicated driver/guide. Premium pricing but with significant flexibility advantages. Best for:

  • Families or groups of 4-8 (per-person cost becomes competitive)
  • Travelers wanting itinerary customization (special interests, specific pacing needs)
  • Travelers with accessibility or mobility considerations
  • Honeymoon trips and couples-focused vacations
  • Travelers with prior travel experience who prefer self-paced engagement

Accommodation tiers

Multi-day tours typically come in three accommodation tiers:

Standard (3-star): Comfortable but unremarkable hotels — Belle Vue Suites Amman, Petra Moon Hotel, basic Wadi Rum Bedouin camps, Holiday Inn Dead Sea. Adequate for travelers prioritizing the destinations over the accommodations. Per-night equivalent: $50-90 per room.

Comfort (4-star): Solid international and Jordanian 4-star properties — Larsa Hotel Amman, Petra Marriott or Mövenpick Resort Petra, comfort-tier Wadi Rum camps, Mövenpick Dead Sea. The most common tier for mid-range packages. Per-night equivalent: $100-180 per room.

Luxury (5-star): Top international properties — Four Seasons Amman, Mövenpick Petra Resort premium suites, luxury Wadi Rum bubble camps, Kempinski Ishtar Dead Sea. Substantial premium but distinctively higher quality. Per-night equivalent: $200-450+ per room.

How to choose an operator

Multiple booking channels with different trade-offs:

Viator and GetYourGuide. The major affiliate-marketed aggregators. Convenient online booking, transparent pricing, customer reviews, standardized cancellation policies. Pricing typically 10-20% above direct booking with Jordanian operators. Best for first-time Jordan bookers without prior local contacts.

Direct booking with Jordanian operators. Major operators include Jordan Inspiration Tours, Petra Nights Tours, Engaging Cultures, and many others. Direct booking saves 15-25% vs aggregators but requires more research and direct communication. The Jordan Tourism Board maintains a list of licensed operators.

Specialized luxury operators. For premium tier travel, specialist operators (Abercrombie & Kent, Audley Travel, Wild Frontiers, regional luxury specialists) offer higher-touch service and customization at premium pricing. Worth the cost for travelers prioritizing the highest-quality experience.

Group tour specialists. Companies like Intrepid Travel, G Adventures, On The Go Tours, and Exodus specialize in shared-group multi-day Middle East tours, with consistent quality and fixed-departure scheduling. Best for solo travelers who specifically want the group format.

Practical considerations

Booking timing. High season (March-May, September-November) bookings should happen 6-12 weeks ahead for best operator choice and hotel availability. Shoulder season (December-February, June-August) bookings can be made 3-6 weeks ahead. Last-minute booking is possible but reduces options.

Cancellation policies. Major operators typically require 25-50% deposit at booking, with full payment 30-60 days before travel. Cancellation policies vary widely; verify before paying deposits. Travel insurance with cancellation coverage is worth the additional cost for tours of this price.

Customization flexibility. Private tours allow significant customization (specific destinations, pacing adjustments, special-interest emphasis). Group tours offer minimal customization but lower pricing. Some operators offer "semi-private" tours (4-8 people in a private vehicle) as a middle option.

Frequently asked questions

What's a multi-day Petra tour?
A tour package combining Petra with other Jordan destinations across multiple days — typically 3-10 days covering some combination of Petra, Wadi Rum, the Dead Sea, Jerash, Amman, and Aqaba. Multi-day formats range from compressed (3 days: Petra + Wadi Rum focus) to comprehensive (7-10 days: full Jordan loop with northern destinations). The format is the most common way for international visitors to experience Jordan, since it amortizes the trip cost across multiple destinations.
What does a multi-day tour cost?
Wide range depending on duration, accommodation tier, and inclusions. Approximate 2026 pricing per person, double occupancy: 3-day Petra+Wadi Rum at $400-700, 5-day Petra+Wadi Rum+Dead Sea at $750-1,400, 7-day full Jordan loop at $1,100-2,200, 10-day comprehensive at $1,500-3,500. Luxury tiers with premium hotels and additional inclusions run 40-80% higher.
What's included?
Standard inclusions for mid-range packages: round-trip airport transfers, private vehicle and English-speaking driver/guide throughout, accommodation at 4-star hotels with breakfast, most lunches at scheduled restaurants, all entrance fees (Jordan Pass typically included for 2+ night stays), Bedouin camp overnight in Wadi Rum (basic or comfort tier), Dead Sea hotel with beach access, and licensed guide for in-site interpretation at major archaeological sites. Drinks, alcohol, optional activities, and gratuities are typically NOT included unless specified.
Private or shared multi-day tour?
Depends on preference and budget. Shared group tours (8-15 people in a coach or large van) run 30-40% cheaper than private equivalents and offer the social experience of traveling with others. Private tours (just your party) offer flexibility, customization, and a more personal pace at premium pricing. For couples and groups of 3-4, private becomes increasingly competitive per-person; for solo travelers and couples on tight budgets, shared group format makes more sense.
When should I book?
For high season (March-May, September-November), 6-12 weeks ahead is the safe window — popular operators and good hotels fill up. For shoulder season (December-February, June-August), 3-6 weeks ahead is usually sufficient. Last-minute booking (less than 2 weeks) is possible but reduces choice on operators and hotels. Multi-day private tours have more booking flexibility than fixed-departure group tours, since private operators schedule around your dates.
