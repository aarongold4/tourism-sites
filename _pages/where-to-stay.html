Where to Stay

Which neighbourhood suits your trip, and the hotels worth booking in each.

Last reviewed June 2026·Editorial standards

Tashkent is large and spread out, so where you stay shapes your days more than in a compact old town. Most visitors choose between the modern centre, around Amir Temur Square, and a base closer to the old city near Chorsu, while the international five-stars cluster in the centre and the newer Tashkent City Park district.

Choosing your area

  • City centre (Amir Temur Square) — the most convenient: walkable to museums and Broadway, best metro links, widest hotel choice. Our default recommendation for a first visit. See the city-centre guide.
  • Near the old city (Chorsu / Eski Shahar) — quieter and often better value, right by the historic sights, with fewer big-brand hotels. See near the old city.
  • Tashkent City Park — modern glass towers (Hilton, Holiday Inn) near shopping and parks, a short hop from the centre.
  • Yunusobod and the north — residential and calmer, good for longer or repeat stays, with the Radisson Blu among the options.

What to expect on price

Tashkent offers strong value. International five-stars like the Hyatt Regency or Hilton cost a fraction of their Western equivalents, while comfortable mid-range chains and well-rated guesthouses near the old city are cheaper still. Whatever you pay, distances are short and taxis are cheap, so no area leaves you stranded.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the best area to stay in Tashkent?

For most first-time visitors, the modern centre around Amir Temur Square — it is walkable to the museums and Broadway, has the best metro links and the widest choice of hotels. Stay near Chorsu for a quieter, more traditional and often cheaper base.

Are hotels in Tashkent expensive?

No — they are good value. International five-stars cost far less than in the West, and mid-range hotels and guesthouses are very affordable. Because the city is compact to get around by cheap taxi, you can prioritise price or character over location.
