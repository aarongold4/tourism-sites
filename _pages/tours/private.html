Private Petra tours

A dedicated guide and vehicle just for you or your group. Ranges from 50 JOD for a half-day in-site guide to $1,500+ per day for luxury multi-destination Jordan tours. Here's what private tours include, when they're worth it, and where to book.

Private tours occupy a significant middle ground between fully DIY Petra travel (rental car, no guide, self-directed exploration) and the most economical group tours (shared bus, fixed itinerary, standard route). The defining feature is exclusivity: you get a guide, often a vehicle, and a customizable itinerary for just yourself or your immediate party. The pricing reflects the exclusivity — private tours cost meaningfully more than group alternatives — but the experience trades cost for flexibility, personalization, and depth.

This page covers what private Petra tours actually include, the pricing tiers at different service levels, when private is worth the premium over alternatives, and where to book licensed operators. The framing throughout: useful if you have a clear preference for personalized travel, less useful if budget is the primary constraint.

What "private" actually means

The "private" designation covers a wide range of arrangements. Three principal types:

Licensed in-site guide only

You arrange your own transport, accommodation, and broader logistics, but hire a licensed Petra guide to walk with you through the site for a half-day or full day. The guide meets you at the Visitor Centre, provides interpretive commentary on the monuments, answers questions, and (in most cases) brings family-level Bedouin knowledge of the site that no guidebook can match. See guided tours for the broader context on hiring guides.

Cost: Official guide rates are 50 JOD for the Main Trail (about 2.5 hours), 80-100 JOD for the Main Trail plus the High Place of Sacrifice and 100 JOD for the Main Trail plus the Monastery (as of Nov 2026). Booked at the Visitor Centre or pre-arranged through hotels.

Day tour with private driver + guide

A car-and-driver-and-guide arrangement covering a full day, usually with a specific destination focus. Common configurations: Day Tour from Amman (driver picks you up at your Amman hotel, drives to Petra, in-site guide handles Petra interpretation, return drive to Amman the same day); or Day Tour from Petra hotel (driver provides transport to side destinations like Little Petra or Shobak, with guide handling interpretation).

Cost: 150-250 USD per day for 1-2 people, including driver, guide, vehicle, fuel, and Petra entrance fees. Lunch may or may not be included; clarify when booking.

Multi-day Jordan tour package

The full private experience. Covers multiple destinations across Jordan over 4-10 days, with all logistics handled by the tour operator: private driver and vehicle, licensed guide where appropriate, pre-booked hotels at predetermined service levels, meals at scheduled restaurants, and entrance fees to all major sites. Common itineraries cover Amman, Jerash, the Dead Sea, Petra, Wadi Rum, and Aqaba.

Cost: 350-600 USD per day per couple for mid-range packages with 4-star hotels; 800-1,500+ USD per day per couple for luxury packages with 5-star hotels (Mövenpick Petra Resort, Mövenpick Tala Bay Aqaba, Kempinski Ishtar Dead Sea, etc.). The cost per person decreases somewhat for larger group bookings since the vehicle and guide costs are shared.

What's included at each tier

Budget tier (150-250 USD/day for 2 people)

  • Driver and vehicle (standard sedan or small SUV)
  • Licensed guide for in-site interpretation
  • Petra entrance fees
  • Mid-range hotel accommodation (3-star, typically 50-80 USD per night)
  • Breakfast at hotels; lunch and dinner at owner's expense

Mid-range tier (350-600 USD/day for 2 people)

  • Driver and vehicle (mid-range SUV)
  • Dedicated tour guide accompanying the full trip (not just in-site)
  • All entrance fees (Petra, Wadi Rum, Jerash, Dead Sea sites, Jordan Pass equivalent)
  • 4-star hotel accommodation (Mövenpick Petra Resort, Petra Marriott, Mövenpick Tala Bay, Kempinski Dead Sea, similar)
  • Breakfast at hotels, most lunches at scheduled restaurants
  • Bedouin camp overnight in Wadi Rum (mid-range or luxury camp)
  • Pre-arranged Petra by Night tickets where included

Luxury tier (800-1,500+ USD/day for 2 people)

  • Premium SUV or van with driver
  • Senior-level licensed guide with specialized knowledge
  • All entrance fees and incidentals
  • 5-star hotel accommodation (Mövenpick Petra Resort premium suites, Kempinski Aqaba, Four Seasons Amman, Ma'In Hot Springs Resort)
  • All meals at premium restaurants
  • Luxury Wadi Rum camp (private tents, gourmet dinner, premium service)
  • Helicopter tour upgrades available (Petra aerial tour, Dead Sea region)
  • Spa and wellness inclusions at applicable hotels
  • Custom photography arrangements, special-access cultural experiences, or other premium add-ons

When private is worth the premium

Private tours make sense in specific situations:

Limited time

Travelers with only 3-5 days for the entire Jordan trip benefit from private logistics — the time saved by not navigating public transport, ad-hoc guide arrangements, and self-directed planning is significant when total time is short. A 4-day private Jordan tour delivers a roughly comparable depth of experience to what 6-7 self-planned days would produce.

Special interests

Photography travelers, biblical-history enthusiasts, geology aficionados, family-history visitors (Jordanian-American families researching ancestral villages), and other specialized travelers benefit substantially from private arrangements. The guide can customize the focus, the pace allows deeper exploration of relevant sites, and the flexibility accommodates the specific interest in ways that group tours don't.

Larger families or groups

For 4-8 people traveling together, the per-person cost of a private vehicle and guide becomes competitive with group tour pricing — and the flexibility advantage is substantial. A family of 6 splitting a 250 USD/day private tour pays roughly 42 USD per person per day, comparable to what a mid-range group tour would cost while delivering significantly more personalized service.

Older travelers and accessibility needs

Travelers who need pacing flexibility, accessible vehicles, or specific accommodation requirements benefit from private arrangements that can be customized to their needs. Group tours work on fixed schedules and standard accessibility levels; private tours adapt.

First-time Middle East travelers

For travelers nervous about navigating Jordan independently, the full private logistics (someone else handles every detail) provides peace of mind worth the premium. Confidence builds over the course of the trip, but having the support structure during the introduction is valuable.

When private isn't worth it

Three traveler profiles where private tours aren't the right choice:

Budget-focused travelers. The cost difference is real — a private Petra tour can cost 5-10x what the same destinations cost with JETT bus + self-directed visits + ad-hoc guide hires. For travelers prioritizing cost, the DIY approach delivers substantial savings without dramatic loss of experience quality.

Solo travelers seeking social experience. Group tours offer the social dimension of traveling with others — meeting fellow visitors, sharing meals, comparing reactions to the monuments. Private tours are isolating by comparison; for travelers valuing the social experience, group tours are preferable.

Standard 2-3 day Petra-only visits. For travelers visiting Petra as part of a broader Israel/Jordan/Egypt trip, with just 2-3 days in Petra itself, the marginal value of private over DIY+ad-hoc guide is limited. Save the private-tour investment for trips with broader Jordan exploration.

Where to book

Affiliate-marketed aggregators

The most convenient starting point for international travelers without prior Jordan contacts. Viator and GetYourGuide are the two major platforms offering Jordan tours with online booking, transparent pricing, customer reviews, and standardized cancellation policies. Prices are typically 10-20% higher than direct booking with local operators, but the convenience and reliability are real.

Search for "private Petra tour" or "private Jordan tour" on these platforms and filter by length, included destinations, and price range.

Direct booking with Jordanian operators

For travelers willing to do more research and direct communication, booking directly with established Jordanian tour operators typically saves 15-25% compared to aggregator pricing. The major operators have multilingual staff, English-language websites, and clear booking processes. The Jordan Tourism Board maintains a list of licensed operators.

Communication is typically by email and WhatsApp; payment is by bank transfer or in-cash on arrival. The trust dimension matters more than with aggregator bookings (you're dealing directly with the operator rather than through a guaranteed platform), but the cost savings can be meaningful for multi-day packages.

Booking through your hotel

Most Wadi Musa hotels can arrange licensed guides, day-tour drivers, and side-trip transportation on request. Prices are typically competitive with direct booking, and the hotel's relationship with local providers provides an informal quality check. For travelers who've already arrived in Petra without pre-arranged guiding, this is often the easiest way to add a guide for the next day.

Selecting your tour

Key questions to ask when comparing private tour options:

  • Is the guide licensed? Petra requires licensed guides for in-site interpretation. Verify license status before booking.
  • What's specifically included in the price? Vehicle, fuel, driver, guide, entrance fees, hotels, meals — get a specific list. "Some meals included" is less clear than "breakfast and lunch each day, dinner on three specified evenings."
  • What's the cancellation policy? Especially for multi-day packages, understand the cancellation terms before paying deposits.
  • Can the itinerary be customized? The principal advantage of private over group is flexibility; verify that the operator is genuinely willing to customize rather than just selling pre-fixed packages.
  • What are the hotel categories? "4-star" can mean very different things across hotels; get specific property names where possible.
  • Are there hidden costs? Petra by Night tickets, optional add-ons, mandatory gratuities, fuel surcharges on long days — make sure you understand the full cost structure before committing.

Frequently asked questions

What's a private Petra tour?
A tour with a dedicated guide and (usually) vehicle, just for you or your group, rather than a shared group tour with strangers. Private tours can range from a single licensed guide hired for a half-day inside Petra (50-100 JOD for the guide, depending on the trail) to multi-day Jordan tours with private driver, guide, and pre-booked hotels (300-600+ USD per day for the full package). Private tours offer flexibility, customization, and personalized attention; group tours offer lower per-person cost and the social experience of traveling with others.
What does a private tour cost?
Wide range depending on what's included. (1) Licensed Petra guide only: the official rates are 50 JOD for the Main Trail, 80-100 JOD with the High Place of Sacrifice and 100 JOD with the Monastery (as of Nov 2026) — guide walks with you through the site, no transport included. (2) Full-day private with driver + guide + lunch: 150-250 USD per day for 1-2 people. (3) Multi-day Jordan private tour (Petra + Wadi Rum + Dead Sea + Jerash): 350-600 USD per day per couple, including driver, guide, hotels, and most meals. (4) Luxury private tours with premium hotels and additional inclusions: 800-1,500+ USD per day per couple.
When is a private tour worth it?
Three situations. (1) Limited time, want to maximize what you see — private guides know the efficient routes and the best timing for each monument. (2) Special interests (photography, geology, biblical history, family history) — private guides can customize the focus. (3) Larger families or groups (4-8 people) where the per-person cost of a private vehicle and guide becomes competitive with group tour pricing. For solo travelers and couples on standard 2-3 day Petra visits, the cost difference compared to DIY+JETT+ad-hoc guide is significant.
What's the difference between a guide and a driver?
A guide is licensed to interpret the site — they walk with you through Petra, explain the monuments, answer questions, and provide cultural context. A driver moves you between locations (Amman→Petra, Petra→Wadi Rum) but doesn't enter Petra with you or interpret the site. Many private tour packages combine both: the driver handles transport between cities, the guide handles in-site interpretation. The distinction matters for pricing: drivers cost 50-80 JOD per day, guides cost 70-150 JOD per day, and combined packages run higher.
Where do I book?
Three main channels. (1) Affiliate-marketed tour aggregators (Viator, GetYourGuide) — convenient online booking, often slightly higher prices, easy comparison shopping, good for first-time bookers. (2) Direct booking with licensed Jordanian tour operators — typically 15-25% cheaper than aggregator pricing, more flexibility for customization, but requires direct contact and trust-building. (3) Booking through your hotel — most Wadi Musa hotels can arrange licensed guides and drivers on request, often at competitive rates. For most international travelers without prior Jordan contacts, the aggregator route is the easier starting point.
Can I customize the itinerary?
Yes — that's the main advantage. Private tour operators are typically very willing to customize: focus on specific monuments (Treasury photography emphasis), add specific destinations (Little Petra, Shobak Castle, side trips to Wadi Mujib), adjust pacing (slow contemplative visit vs. high-energy maximum-coverage), include or exclude specific activities (Petra by Night, Bedouin tea stops, Monastery climb), and accommodate dietary or accessibility requirements. The customization is the principal value proposition compared to group tours.
