Privacy policy

How we handle information about visitors to PetraTourism.com — what we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights.

Last reviewed April 28, 2026 · See our editorial standards

Effective date: April 28, 2026. Last updated: April 28, 2026.

This privacy policy describes how PetraTourism.com collects, uses, and shares information about visitors to this website. By using the site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information we collect

Information collected automatically

When you visit PetraTourism.com, our servers and our analytics providers automatically log certain information about your visit, including:

  • IP address (anonymized before storage where technically feasible)
  • Browser type and version, operating system, device type
  • Referring website or search query that brought you to our site
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and click paths within the site
  • Approximate geographic location (typically city or region level, derived from IP address)

We collect this information through Google Analytics 4. Google's own privacy policy governs their use of this data.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email or fill out a form on the site, we collect the information you provide directly, including your email address and any message contents. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and do not share it with third parties unless required by law.

Information collected through affiliate networks

When you click an affiliate link on our site (see our affiliate disclosure), the affiliate network sets a cookie that allows them to attribute any subsequent booking to PetraTourism.com. We do not have direct access to the contents of these cookies, but the affiliate networks may collect information about your behavior on their own sites in connection with these cookies. Their privacy policies govern their use of this data.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies — required for basic site functionality
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics 4, to understand how readers use the site
  • Advertising cookies — Google AdSense, where ads appear, to serve relevant advertising
  • Affiliate tracking cookies — set by our affiliate partners when you click outbound links

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. If you do, some site features may not work as expected. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve PetraTourism.com
  • Understand how readers use the site and which content is most useful
  • Respond to reader inquiries and correspondence
  • Serve advertising through Google AdSense
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal information to third parties. We do not share email addresses or contact information with affiliate partners or advertisers.

Your rights — GDPR (European Economic Area)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have certain rights under the General Data Protection Regulation, including:

  • The right to access information we hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information
  • The right to request deletion of your information ("right to be forgotten")
  • The right to object to processing of your information
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to withdraw consent for processing that requires consent

To exercise any of these rights, write to privacy@petratourism.com. We respond to verified requests within 30 days as required by GDPR.

Your rights — CCPA (California residents)

If you are a California resident, you have certain rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including:

  • The right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it
  • The right to request deletion of personal information
  • The right to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information, so this right does not currently apply)
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

To exercise any of these rights, write to privacy@petratourism.com.

Children's privacy

PetraTourism.com is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact privacy@petratourism.com and we will delete it.

Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide the services you have requested or as required by applicable law. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' standard retention settings (currently 14 months) unless we configure shorter retention.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will provide additional notice on the homepage or via email if we have your email address.

Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or other concerns about how we handle your information, write to privacy@petratourism.com.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as pages visited and time on site; this data is reported to us in aggregate. You can prevent Google Analytics from using your data with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
