Corrections are the most valuable mail we get.

Last reviewed June 6, 2026

The short version: one email address, read by the people who maintain the pages, with corrections answered fastest of all — because a guide that asks to be held accountable has to make accountability easy.

Spotted an outdated price, a changed opening hour, a closed entrance, or anything else on this site that doesn't match what you found on the ground? Email hello@gizatourism.com. Corrections are verified against official sources and current reports, applied to the relevant page, and stamped with an updated review date — usually within days. If you tell us roughly when you visited, verification goes faster.

The same address handles everything else: general questions about planning a visit (we read everything, and recurring questions shape what gets written next), feedback on the site, and partnership or press inquiries. What we don't do: sell placements, accept payment for recommendations, or publish sponsored content presented as editorial — the rules are in our editorial standards and affiliate disclosure.

What makes a correction fast to act on, if you want to help us help the next traveler: the page URL, what the page says, what you found instead, roughly when you were there, and — gold, when you have it — a photo of the posted price board or ticket. With those five things a fix usually ships the same week. Without them we can still verify, just slower. Anonymous corrections are fine; we never publish correspondents' names or details, and your email is used only to reply (per the privacy policy).

Response expectations, honestly set: correction reports and factual questions get answered, usually within a few days; planning questions get read and often shape future pages even when a personal reply isn't possible; press and partnership inquiries get answered when they fit the site's rules. What never gets a reply: link-exchange spam, "guest post" offers, and anything proposing paid placement in editorial content — the standards page explains why that's a permanent no.

One thing this page can't be: a concierge. We can't book tours, confirm individual reservations, or answer same-day "is the site open right now" questions — for those, the official ticketing platform at egymonuments.com and your operator are the right doors.

This page is maintained by the Giza Tourism editorial team and reviewed on a rolling basis as prices, opening hours, and ticket policies change. Last reviewed June 6, 2026. Spotted something out of date? Tell us and we'll verify and correct it.
