About PetraTourism.com

Why this site exists

Petra is one of the most-searched destinations in the world, and one of the most poorly served by the English-language travel web. Search "best Petra tours" and you'll get a mix of recycled guidebook excerpts, hotel chains' marketing pages, and aggregator sites that have clearly never been there.

PetraTourism.com is a small, independent publication built around a single goal: be the resource we couldn't find ourselves. Practical, opinionated, and updated.

How we work

Every tour, hotel, monument, and trail covered on this site has been seen firsthand by someone on our editorial team. Where we lean on outside expertise — local guides, archaeologists, hoteliers — we name them. We don't fabricate experiences and we don't recycle press kits.

We pay for tours and accommodations at full retail, the same way you would, and we book anonymously. On the rare occasion an operator hosts us, we say so on the page in question. We never accept payment for positive coverage, and operators don't review our work before it's published.

Every page on the site shows when it was last reviewed. Tour pricing, opening hours, and entrance fees move — we re-check every monetized page at least every six months, and more often when we have reason to.

How we make money

PetraTourism.com is funded by two things: affiliate commissions from tour and hotel bookings (when you book through a link on the site, we earn a small percentage at no cost to you) and display advertising on a subset of pages. Affiliate relationships do not determine which tours or hotels we recommend. If a better option costs less and pays us nothing, that's still the option we recommend.

For the full picture, see our editorial standards and affiliate disclosure.

What we don't cover

We're a Petra-and-Jordan publication. We don't pretend to be authorities on neighboring countries we haven't worked the same way. When our itineraries cross borders into Israel or Egypt, we point readers to specialists for the parts of the trip we can't speak to from experience.

Get in touch

Reach the editors at hello@petratourism.com for tips, corrections, story leads, or accessibility questions. We read everything, even when we can't reply quickly.
