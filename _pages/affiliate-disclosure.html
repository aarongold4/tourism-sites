Affiliate disclosure

How PetraTourism.com makes money, which programs we work with, and why affiliate relationships do not change what we recommend.

Last reviewed April 28, 2026 · See our editorial standards

Why this page exists

PetraTourism.com is an independent publication funded primarily by reader bookings made through affiliate links on this site. We are required by the United States Federal Trade Commission to disclose this commercial relationship clearly to readers, and we believe in disclosing it whether or not we are required to.

What an affiliate link is

When we recommend a tour, a hotel, a travel insurance policy, or another bookable product, the link to that product often passes through an affiliate network. If you click the link and complete a booking, we receive a small commission from the operator or platform. The commission does not increase the price you pay — you book at the same rate you would by going to the operator's website directly.

We work with the following affiliate programs as of the most recent update to this page:

  • Viator (a Tripadvisor company) — tour and activity bookings
  • GetYourGuide — tour and activity bookings
  • Booking.com — hotel bookings
  • SafetyWing and similar — travel insurance
  • Amazon Associates — books, gear, and travel accessories where mentioned

We may add additional affiliate programs over time. This page is updated when new programs are added.

How affiliate relationships affect our coverage

They do not. Our editorial standards prohibit affiliate relationships from influencing which tours, hotels, or products we recommend. We recommend the option we believe is best for the reader given the criteria of the page — value, convenience, suitability for the traveler we're writing for — regardless of whether a particular operator pays us a commission.

If a better tour costs less and pays us nothing, that is still the tour we recommend, and we will say so plainly. We've structured our recommendation methodology specifically to make this distinction clear, and we would rather lose affiliate revenue than misrepresent what we believe is the right choice for our readers.

How to support PetraTourism.com (or not)

If you find our writing useful and you book a tour, hotel, or product we cover, using the links on the site is a meaningful way to support our work at no cost to yourself. If you prefer not to use affiliate links — for any reason at all — you can go directly to the relevant operator's website and book there. We do not track or judge readers who book outside our affiliate links.

Cookies

Some affiliate networks use cookies to track that a booking originated from a click on PetraTourism.com. These cookies are described in our privacy policy. You can decline these cookies in your browser settings without affecting your ability to use the site or to book through any affiliate partner directly.

Display advertising

In addition to affiliate links, some pages on PetraTourism.com display advertising through Google AdSense. Advertising revenue and editorial recommendations are independent — we have no input into which specific ads are served on any given page, and Google's advertisers have no editorial relationship with us.

Contact

For questions about our affiliate relationships or advertising, write to editor@petratourism.com.
