The Balkans, before everyone finds out

North Macedonia packs an outsized amount into a country you can drive across in three hours: a UNESCO-listed lake older than almost any in Europe, a capital that mixes Ottoman bazaars with brutalist monuments, three national parks, cliff-hung monasteries and a wine region most travelers have never heard of. It is also one of the cheapest destinations on the continent — and the buses still run on time.

New here? Start with the complete North Macedonia travel guide — the whole country on one page.
